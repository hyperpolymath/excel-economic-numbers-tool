# Academic Partnership Program - v10.0

## Mission

Partner with universities and research institutions worldwide to:
- Advance economic research capabilities
- Train next generation of economists
- Validate toolkit with rigorous academic standards
- Contribute improvements back to open source

## Partnership Types

### 1. Research Partner

**Benefits for Institution**:
- Free Enterprise license for research projects
- Direct support channel
- Early access to experimental features
- Co-author opportunities on research papers
- Annual workshops at your institution

**Requirements**:
- Active economic research program
- Commitment to publish results with toolkit
- Cite toolkit in publications
- Provide feedback on features
- Allow case studies

**Examples**:
- MIT Economics Department
- London School of Economics
- University of Chicago Booth
- Max Planck Institute for Economics

### 2. Curriculum Partner

**Benefits for Institution**:
- Free licenses for students and faculty
- Custom course materials
- Teaching workshops for faculty
- Student internship pipeline
- Co-branded certification

**Requirements**:
- Integrate toolkit into curriculum
- Minimum 2 courses using toolkit
- Train faculty on toolkit
- Share course materials (optionally)
- Provide student feedback

**Suggested Courses**:
- Econometrics I & II
- Applied Time Series
- Development Economics
- International Economics
- Financial Economics

### 3. Student Program

**Benefits for Students**:
- Free Professional licenses
- Career development workshops
- Job board access
- Mentorship opportunities
- Conference scholarships

**Requirements**:
- Enrolled in partner institution
- Economics, statistics, or related major
- Complete foundation certification
- Participate in community
- Share projects (optionally)

### 4. Research Computing Partner

**Benefits for Institution**:
- Dedicated compute resources
- Custom data integrations
- Technical support
- Plugin development assistance
- Infrastructure consulting

**Requirements**:
- Large-scale research projects
- High-performance computing needs
- Willing to open source improvements
- Present findings at conferences
- Contribute to benchmarks

## Application Process

### 1. Initial Inquiry

Email: academic@economictoolkit.org

Include:
- Institution name and department
- Partnership type interested in
- Brief description of use case
- Expected scale (students, faculty, projects)
- Point of contact

### 2. Proposal Review

Timeline: 2-4 weeks

Review by:
- Community team
- Technical team (if applicable)
- Steering Committee (for major partnerships)

### 3. Agreement

If approved:
- Sign partnership agreement
- Set up licenses and access
- Schedule kickoff meeting
- Assign liaison from our team

### 4. Onboarding

Duration: 1-2 months

Activities:
- Technical setup
- Faculty/staff training
- Student onboarding
- Documentation review
- Support channel setup
